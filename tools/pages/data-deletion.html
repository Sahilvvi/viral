Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email {{site.privacy_email}} from the address on your account, with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. We’ll confirm it’s you, then delete your account, briefs and enquiries within 30 days.
  3. We’ll email you when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in details
  • Briefs and reference links you submitted
  • Enquiries and newsletter subscription

What we may keep

Invoices and payment records, which we must retain under tax law, and delivered work that belongs to you. Backups roll off within 30 days.

To unsubscribe from the newsletter only, reply “unsubscribe” to any issue, or email us.

See also our Privacy Policy.
