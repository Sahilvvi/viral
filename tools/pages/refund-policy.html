Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated {{site.legal_updated}}

Every project is custom work, so refunds depend on how far the project has progressed. Here is how it works.

Before work starts

If you cancel before we begin production, we refund the advance in full, less any payment-processing fees.

After work starts

Stage at cancellationWhat you receive
Concept & scriptRefund of the advance less the script stage cost
Storyboard & lookRefund of the advance less the work completed, and you keep the storyboard
Production & postNo refund of completed stages. You may collect the work completed so far
Master deliveredNo refund. The project is complete

Revisions

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds. Extra rounds are quoted separately. Revisions are for changes within the agreed brief and not for changing the concept after approval.

If we miss what we promised

If we don’t deliver what the quote describes, we’ll fix it at no cost. If we can’t, we refund the affected part of the project.

How to request one

Email {{site.support_email}} with your project name. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
