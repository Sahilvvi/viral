Pricing questions

Why custom quotes instead of fixed prices?

A phone launch with a hero 3D model is a different job from a serum drop or a SaaS reveal. Quoting after a short call means you pay for the shots your product actually needs, not a one-size-fits-all bundle.

How long does a launch film take?

Most launch films go from brief to master delivery in 5–7 days. Reels and teasers can land sooner.

What do you need from me to quote?

Your product, the launch date, where it will run (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, ads) and any reference you like. Photos or a CAD/3D file help if you have them.

Can I change the package later?

Yes. Many clients start with a Launch Reel and upgrade to a Launch Film or Campaign. We credit relevant work already delivered.

Do you work with international brands?

Yes. We work remotely and deliver through a secure transfer. International quotes are in USD.
